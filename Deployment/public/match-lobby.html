📋 Rules & Regulations Full details of this game — how to play, entry, winning & fair play ⌄

🎮 How to play

  • Pick a room based on the number of players and the entry fee, then tap Join & Play.
  • The server puts you into a room and the real Unity match starts once the room is full.
  • It's a skill-based tile race — match tiles faster and score higher than your opponents.
  • The result depends only on skill & speed — there is no luck-based outcome.

💰 Entry & wallet

  • One WXO account & wallet works across every game.
  • The entry fee is deducted from your WXO wallet the moment you join a room.
  • 1 WXO Coin = ₹1. Buy coins from Wallet → Deposit.
  • Free Practice rooms cost 0 and pay 0 — for warm-up only.

🏆 Winning & rewards

  • The winner gets 2× the entry fee in WXO coins. All other players get 0.
  • In multiplayer rooms, only the top-ranked (1st place) player wins.
  • Rewards are credited to your wallet automatically right after the match ends.

🔴 Live stream rooms

  • Rooms marked LIVE may be streamed and watched by other users.
  • The same entry and winning rules apply as normal rooms.

⚖️ Fair play

  • One account per person. Multiple accounts or collusion leads to a ban and forfeiture of coins.
  • Bots, hacks, or exploiting bugs are strictly prohibited.
  • Leaving mid-match counts as a loss; the entry fee is not refunded.
  • Internet/connection issues at your end are your own responsibility.

💳 Withdrawals & coins

  • Eligible earned coins can be withdrawn per platform policy via Swap / Wallet.
  • Deposit or bonus coins may carry withdrawal conditions.

✅ Eligibility

  • You must be 18+ and in a region where skill-based gaming is permitted.
  • By joining a match you agree to the WXO Terms & Privacy Policy.
